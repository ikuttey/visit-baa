A remarkable place

A travel hub shaped around the atoll itself.

Baa Atoll is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve in the central-western Maldives. UNESCO describes it as supporting one of the largest groups of coral reefs in the Indian Ocean. The platform can celebrate that natural identity while keeping local businesses and island communities at the center of the visitor journey.
